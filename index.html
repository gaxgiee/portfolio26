Case study · Service design

Ending the nightly price-label shift

How time on the shop floor turned a manual night routine into one price update that reaches every branch.

RoleProduct Design Manager
ScopeStore operations · Back office
MethodsField research · Shadowing
01Problem

Every night, staff changed prices by hand

Each promotion meant printing new labels and swapping them shelf by shelf after closing. The same SKU could show different prices in different branches, and customers noticed.

02Research

I worked the night shift

Instead of reading reports, I went into stores and did the job with the team, to see where the routine broke down.

BeforePromo list arrives
Print new labels
Find each shelfPain point
Swap labels by handPain point
Fix mismatchesPain point
03Insights

What we learned

The problem was the system, not the staff

Manual updates across many branches will always drift, however careful people are.

Night work was the hidden cost

Re-labelling took staff time every night that could go to customers and stock.

04Design

One update, every branch

I designed the service around digital shelf labels: a promotion is set once, and every branch that sells the same SKU updates in real time.

AfterPromotion set once
Synced by SKU
Labels update in every branch
Staff handle exceptions only
05Results

What changed

Promotions now reach every shelf tag in real time. Staff no longer spend the night swapping paper labels, and the same product shows the same price in every branch.

For customers

The price on the shelf matches the price at the till.

For staff and the business

Night work goes to customers and stock, and promotions launch the moment they're decided.
